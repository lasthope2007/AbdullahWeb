01THE STORY

Built around the
rhythm of a match.

Karate Scoreboard started with a simple goal: make Kumite match management easier to follow. Timing, scoring and match state should be clear at a glance, while the referee stays in control of the flow.

The result is an Android application designed around a focused, competition-inspired interface. This page will grow alongside the product with real screenshots, release notes and future updates.

02KEY CAPABILITIES
01

Match timer

Manual start, pause and resume controls keep the referee in charge of the clock.

02

Scorekeeping

Clear Aka and Ao scoring with a match-focused visual hierarchy.

03

Match flow

Winner and end-of-match handling designed to keep the result easy to understand.

04

Local history

Offline-first direction with local match history and practical on-device use.
